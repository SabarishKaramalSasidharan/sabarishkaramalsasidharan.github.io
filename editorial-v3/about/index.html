From engineering to design

I started as a Programmer Analyst working in performance engineering at Cognizant, then studied Information Design at NID. Today at Salesforce, I design how teams build and use AI models.

Performance engineering taught me to trace where a bottleneck begins and notice how it affects the rest of a system.

How I work

  1. 01

    I question the brief.

  2. 02

    I talk to the people who'll use it.

  3. 03

    I explore wide before I go deep.

  4. 04

    I prototype the hard parts first.

  5. 05

    I ship, then I measure.

  6. 06

    I do it again.

In Model Builder, usability testing showed that people missed the settings behind field names. That finding changed what I worked on first. I redesigned how people opened the settings as well as how they edited them.
