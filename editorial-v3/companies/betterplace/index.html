BetterPlace

I led design across B2B and employee-facing products for frontline workforce management.

Tools for frontline work

BetterPlace built a platform for employers managing frontline teams across multiple locations. It brought together hiring, background checks, onboarding, attendance, and payroll. It also offered workers access to services such as training and insurance.
