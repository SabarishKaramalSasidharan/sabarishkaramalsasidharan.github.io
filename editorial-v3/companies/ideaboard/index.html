Graduation project · National Institute of Design · Jun 2019 – Dec 2019

IdeaBoard

For my M.Des graduation project, I worked with a Bengaluru edtech team to build on its assessment platform with new tools for families, students, and school staff.

How schools used IdeaBoard

Teachers made exams in the Question Bank, students practised and took tests in the Student App, and deans reviewed results in the Dashboard. I studied where those tools fit into school life and where people still needed a way to do their work.

📊Product analytics
I used Amplitude to see where users exited or dropped off, then interviewed users to understand what happened at those points.
💬Client interviews
I met existing clients with the business development team to hear where IdeaBoard fit into their work and where they needed more support.
📋Questionnaires
I asked what people came to do, whether they could complete it, how they had done it before, and what they were missing.

Where the platform fell short

  • Parents lacked access to results

    Parents had no direct way to see their child’s exam results or understand how they had performed.

  • Paper exams stayed offline

    Institutions that hadn’t purchased the Student App couldn’t bring paper answer sheets into IdeaBoard for analysis.

  • Administrators needed their own tools

    The platform had no dedicated way for administrators to manage students.

The proposed ecosystem

I mapped five additions around the assessment tools already in place. The goal was to make results accessible to parents, bring paper exams into online reporting, and give students and school staff tools for their day-to-day work.

What I designed

Parents Portal

Progressive web app for parents

I designed a portal where parents could see their child’s results as soon as they were available. Each exam had a report with a detailed performance breakdown.

OMR Scanner

Mobile app for junior lecturers and administrators

I designed a way for staff to scan and upload paper answer sheets from a phone. The scans could feed into IdeaBoard’s existing exam analysis, including for institutions without the Student App.

Formulae Tool

Revision feature in the Student App

I designed an organised space for students to review formulae alongside the app’s existing test practice and personalised assessments.

Student Management

Web app for school administrators

I designed a dedicated web app for institutions to manage their students, extending IdeaBoard’s scope from assessments into day-to-day administration.

Hostel Management

Progressive web app for hostel staff

I designed a workspace for wardens and hostel facilitators to handle issues raised by students and approve leave requests.

IdeaBoard website

Responsive web design

I designed the company’s public site end to end for desktop and mobile. The site was in development when I documented the graduation project.
